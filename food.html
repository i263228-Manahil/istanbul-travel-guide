A Few Local Favourites

From a quick bite in the street to a sweet treat with tea, Istanbul's food is full of flavour and tradition.

Turkish Kebabs

Seasoned meat grilled over a flame, often served with warm bread, rice, and fresh vegetables. Each region has its own delicious style.

Baklava

Thin, crisp layers of pastry filled with nuts and finished with fragrant syrup make this a rich, flaky dessert.

Turkish Delight

Known locally as lokum, these soft, chewy sweets come in flavours such as rose, citrus, and pistachio.

Simit

This round, sesame-crusted bread is a beloved street snack, especially tasty fresh from a bakery with a cup of tea.

Turkish Tea

Strong black tea is traditionally served hot in small tulip-shaped glasses and shared throughout the day.
